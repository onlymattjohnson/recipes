# Deeply Roasted Cabbage With Peanut Crunch

Slow-roasted cabbage wedges with a tangy tamarind finish and a crunchy peanut, sesame, chile, and date topping.

Serves 6 to 8.

Source: [Rainbow Plant Life video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtSO3K61Quo) and [NYT Cooking recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/769796476-deeply-roasted-cabbage-with-peanut-crunch)

## Ingredients

### Roasted Cabbage

* 1 medium green cabbage, 2 to 2 1/2 lb
* 4 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
* Kosher salt
* Freshly ground black pepper

### Peanut Crunch

* 1/2 cup roasted, salted peanuts or cashews, finely chopped
* 2 tbsp white sesame seeds
* 1 1/2 tbsp neutral-flavored oil, such as avocado oil
* 2 tsp black or brown mustard seeds
* 15 fresh curry leaves, optional
* 4 large garlic cloves, finely chopped
* 1/2 to 1 serrano chile, or 1 Thai chile, finely chopped
* 4 large Medjool dates, pitted and finely chopped
* 1 large lime, zested and juiced
* Kosher salt

### To Finish

* 1 tbsp Indian tamarind paste or concentrate, or to taste

## Instructions

1. Place racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Remove loose outer cabbage leaves. Keep the core intact and cut the cabbage into eight equal wedges.
3. Spread 2 tbsp olive oil over a rimmed sheet pan. Sprinkle with 1/2 tsp kosher salt and plenty of black pepper.
4. Place the wedges cut-side down on the pan and rub them in the seasoned oil. Space them apart.
5. Brush the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil over the tops and sides. Season with a heaping 1/2 tsp kosher salt and more pepper.
6. Roast on the lower rack for 50 to 55 minutes, until the bottoms are well browned and the tops are moderately browned.
7. Increase the oven to 425°F. Move the cabbage to the upper rack and roast for another 10 to 15 minutes, until deeply browned and lightly charred. A paring knife should slide into the thickest part with little resistance.
8. While the cabbage roasts, heat a medium frying pan over medium heat. Toast the peanuts and sesame seeds for 3 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Transfer them to a medium bowl.
9. Wipe out the pan and return it to medium heat. Add the neutral oil.
10. Add the mustard seeds. When they begin to pop, add the curry leaves, if using. Stand back because they can sputter. Stir for 15 seconds.
11. Add the garlic and chile. Stir constantly for 1 minute, until the garlic just begins to color. Immediately pour the mixture over the nuts and seeds.
12. Mix in the dates, lime zest, 1 tbsp lime juice, and a pinch of salt. Add more lime juice or salt to taste.
13. Let the cabbage cool for a few minutes, then arrange it on a serving platter.
14. Drizzle with tamarind and more lime juice until tart and bright. Scatter the peanut crunch over the top.

## Notes

* Indian tamarind paste or concentrate is different from sweetened tamarind chutney.

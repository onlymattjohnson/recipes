# Creamy Cabbage Curry

Silky cabbage, chickpeas, and tomatoes simmered with Indian spices, coconut milk, and nut butter.

Serves 4.

Source: [Rainbow Plant Life video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLUvYlDuvmo) and [Rainbow Plant Life recipe](https://rainbowplantlife.com/cabbage-curry/)

## Ingredients

* 2 tbsp (28g) neutral oil, such as avocado oil
* 4 large garlic cloves (21g), chopped
* 1 to 3 serrano peppers, finely chopped
* 8 cups (400 to 480g) green cabbage, shredded or thinly sliced
* Kosher salt
* Freshly cracked black pepper
* 2 tbsp (30g) tomato paste
* 2 tsp Kashmiri chili powder
* 1 1/2 tsp ground coriander
* 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
* 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
* 8 oz (227g) ripe tomatoes, finely chopped
* 1 (13.5 oz / 400 mL) can full-fat coconut milk
* 1 (15 oz / 425g) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
* 2 tbsp (32g) unsweetened almond butter or cashew butter
* 1 tsp garam masala
* 1 lime, juiced
* 1 cup (16g) chopped cilantro leaves and tender stems

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a 12-inch sauté pan over medium-high heat until shimmering.
2. Add the garlic and serrano peppers. Stir frequently for 30 to 60 seconds, until fragrant but not browned.
3. Add the cabbage and toss well to coat it in the oil and aromatics. Season with 1/2 tsp kosher salt.
4. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the cabbage is tender, silky, and browned in spots. If it browns too quickly, reduce the heat or deglaze with a splash of water.
5. While the cabbage cooks, combine the tomato paste, Kashmiri chili powder, coriander, cumin, turmeric, 1 tsp kosher salt, and plenty of black pepper.
6. Add the spice mixture to the cabbage. Stir frequently for 2 minutes, adding a splash of water if the pan becomes dry.
7. Add the tomatoes and cook for 4 to 5 minutes, until soft and nearly melted into the cabbage.
8. Add the coconut milk, chickpeas, nut butter, and 1/2 tsp kosher salt.
9. Simmer uncovered for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring every few minutes. For a thinner curry, add 1/4 to 1/2 cup water.
10. Remove from the heat. Stir in the garam masala, several squeezes of lime juice, and the cilantro.
11. Add more salt or lime juice to taste. Serve with cooked rice or flatbread.

## Notes

* One small cabbage weighing 1 1/4 to 1 1/2 lb typically yields 8 cups when sliced.
* If using table salt or sea salt instead of Diamond Crystal kosher salt, start with about half as much.
* Replace the Kashmiri chili powder with 1 3/4 tsp paprika plus 1/4 tsp cayenne. For a mild version, use 2 tsp paprika.
* Eight ounces of tomatoes is about two medium Roma tomatoes. Canned crushed tomatoes can be substituted by weight.
* For a nut-free curry, replace the almond or cashew butter with tahini.
* Leftovers thicken in the refrigerator. Add water when reheating.

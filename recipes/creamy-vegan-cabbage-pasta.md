# Creamy Vegan Cabbage Pasta

Silky sautéed cabbage and pasta in a savory walnut, oat-milk, and nutritional-yeast sauce.

Serves 4 to 6.

Source: [Rainbow Plant Life video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtSO3K61Quo) and [NYT Cooking recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/769793550-creamy-vegan-cabbage-pasta)

## Ingredients

* 1 small green cabbage, about 1 1/2 lb
* 5 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
* Kosher salt
* Freshly ground black pepper
* 4 large garlic cloves, sliced as thinly as possible
* 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves, roughly chopped
* 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper
* 1 lb bucatini or rigatoni
* 1 1/4 cups full-fat oat milk or cashew milk
* 1/2 cup nutritional yeast
* 3/4 cup walnuts (3 oz), ground into a coarse powder
* 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
* 1 cup fresh basil, chopped or thinly sliced
* Flaky sea salt

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil for the pasta.
2. Quarter and core the cabbage. Slice it very thinly with a mandoline or sharp knife to yield about 10 cups.
3. Heat 3 tbsp olive oil in a 12-inch sauté pan over medium-high heat.
4. Add the cabbage, 1 tsp kosher salt, and plenty of black pepper. Toss to coat, then cook for about 10 minutes. Stir only occasionally so the cabbage becomes tender, silky, and browned in spots. If it browns too quickly, lower the heat and deglaze with a splash of water.
5. Add the garlic, thyme, and crushed red pepper. Stir frequently for 2 minutes, until aromatic and the garlic begins to color. Remove from the heat.
6. Salt the boiling water generously. Add the pasta and cook for the minimum package time, until barely al dente. Reserve a ladleful of pasta water, drain the pasta, and return it to the pot.
7. While the pasta cooks, whisk together the plant milk, nutritional yeast, 1/2 tsp kosher salt, and the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil.
8. Add the sauce and ground walnuts to the pasta. Toss over medium-high heat for 1 to 2 minutes, until the sauce coats the pasta and the pasta is al dente. Add reserved pasta water as needed to help the sauce cling.
9. Fold in the cooked cabbage.
10. Remove from the heat. Stir in the lemon juice and a generous amount of black pepper.
11. Serve with basil, flaky sea salt, and additional black pepper.

## Notes

* Refrigerate leftovers for 3 to 5 days.

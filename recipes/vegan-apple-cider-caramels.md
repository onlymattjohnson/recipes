# Apple cider caramels

Source: [allie’s cottage — apple cider caramels](https://www.tiktok.com/@misshoneyscottage_/video/7680306276768779551), shared by Jill.

## Ingredients — proposed vegan version
- 1 cup (240 ml) apple cider
- 1 tsp apple pie spice (cinnamon, allspice, nutmeg and cloves)
- 1 lb (454 g) salted vegan butter sticks, plus a little for greasing
- 2 cups (400 g) brown sugar
- Dash of salt
- 2 cups (480 ml) light corn syrup (e.g. Karo)
- 396 g sweetened condensed coconut milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Optional flaky sea salt

## Vegan adaptation / shopping notes
Shopping option found by Matt (September 5, 2026): Andre Prost sweetened condensed coconut milk — $3.33 at Walmart in Mount Prospect, aisle A14. Can size: 11.6 oz / 330 g. Buy TWO cans ($6.66 at Matt’s observed price); use 396 g total = one full can plus 66 g from the second. Do not use both full cans.

Replace the dairy butter with vegan baking sticks and the dairy condensed milk with sweetened condensed coconut milk. This is a proposed adaptation of the creator’s dairy recipe, not a tested vegan recipe; texture and setting may differ.
The caption says “1 can (15 oz / 396 g)”; these measures conflict (396 g is approximately 14 oz). Use the stated 396 g weight as the working assumption.
[Nature’s Charm sweetened condensed coconut milk](https://naturescharmmarket.com/products/sweetened-condensed-coconut-milk) is sold in 320 g cans: buy TWO and weigh out 396 g (one can plus 76 g). Choose the regular sweetened product, not unsweetened coconut milk, evaporated coconut milk or sugar-free condensed milk. Meijer availability was not confirmed.
Product suitability checked September 5, 2026: [Andre Prost product listing](https://www.walmart.com/ip/165316733). The cane sugar, water, coconut cream and xanthan gum formula is suitable for this vegan substitution. Retailer ingredient lists differ, so compare the can’s label. [Allergy Awesomeness’s tested chewy vegan caramels](https://allergyawesomeness.com/dairy-free-caramels/) specifically use Andre Prost sweetened condensed coconut milk with vegan butter, corn syrup and brown sugar. This supports using this brand for caramel, but does not validate the exact apple-cider recipe’s proportions or final texture.
Use vegan-labeled brown sugar if sugar processing matters to your vegan preference.

## Equipment
[Mainstays Candy Thermometer — Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/ip/16656322593): $10.26, Mount Prospect Supercenter, aisle BK2; showed pickup available September 5, 2026. Pot clip; 100–400°F range covers the recipe’s 245°F target.

Candy thermometer, large 3–6 qt saucepan (allow room for bubbling), small pot, silicone spatula, 9×13-inch pan, parchment, wax paper wrappers, knife/bench scraper.

## Instructions — adapted from caption
1. Simmer cider in a small pot over medium heat until reduced to 2–3 tbsp (creator estimates 12–18 minutes). Let cool slightly.
2. Line the 9×13 pan with parchment and lightly grease with vegan butter.
3. Melt vegan butter in the large saucepan over medium heat.
4. Stir in brown sugar and salt, then corn syrup. Gradually incorporate condensed coconut milk.
5. Stir constantly with a candy thermometer attached until 245°F (118°C), the creator’s target for the original recipe.
6. Remove from heat; stir in vanilla and apple pie spice. Wait about 1 minute, then carefully stir in the reduced cider. The creator advises letting the cider sit off heat 1–2 minutes before adding to limit splattering.
7. Pour into the pan and optionally sprinkle with flaky salt.
8. Cool completely, lift out, cut and wrap.

The creator lists 2–3 weeks airtight at room temperature or up to 2 months frozen for the dairy version; those storage claims have not been validated for this coconut adaptation.

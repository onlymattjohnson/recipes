# Charred Cabbage in Spiced Tomatoes With Tahini

Deeply browned cabbage wedges braised in a warm tomato-spice sauce and finished with lemony tahini.

Serves 4.

Source: [Rainbow Plant Life video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtSO3K61Quo) and [NYT Cooking recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/769798574-charred-cabbage-in-spiced-tomatoes-with-tahini)

## Ingredients

### Cabbage and Tomato Sauce

* 1/2 medium green or red cabbage, about 1 lb
* 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
* 1 tsp kosher salt, divided
* 1 tbsp pure maple syrup
* 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes, or 1/2 of a 28 oz can whole peeled tomatoes crushed by hand
* 1 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
* 2 medium shallots, sliced
* 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
* 1 tsp paprika
* 1/2 tsp ground coriander
* 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
* 1/8 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
* 1 tbsp Aleppo pepper or Urfa biber, or about 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes

### Tahini Sauce

* 1/4 cup well-stirred tahini
* 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
* 1 tsp pure maple syrup
* 1 garlic clove, grated or pressed
* 1/8 tsp ground cumin
* 1/4 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
* Freshly ground black pepper
* 3 to 4 tbsp ice water, plus more as needed

### To Serve

* 1/2 cup chopped cilantro or flat-leaf parsley leaves and tender stems
* Fresh lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Cut the cabbage half lengthwise through the core to make four wedges.
2. Heat a large, lidded cast-iron skillet or other heavy pan over medium-high heat. Add 2 tbsp olive oil.
3. Place the cabbage cut-side down in the hot oil. Season with 1/2 tsp salt and sear for 5 to 7 minutes, until well browned.
4. Flip the wedges, season with the remaining 1/2 tsp salt, and brown the second side for 5 to 7 minutes. Transfer the cabbage to a plate. Remove the pan from the heat and let it cool for 5 to 10 minutes.
5. Mix 1 tbsp maple syrup into the tomatoes.
6. Return the pan to medium heat and add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil. Toast the cumin seeds for about 1 minute.
7. Add the shallots and garlic. Cook for 2 minutes, until the shallots begin to soften.
8. Add the paprika, coriander, cinnamon, nutmeg, and Aleppo pepper. Stir for 1 minute, adding a little oil if the pan becomes dry.
9. Reduce the heat to medium-low. Add the tomatoes and their juices, then nestle the cabbage wedges into the sauce.
10. Cover and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes, until the cabbage is tender and the sauce has thickened. Add a splash of water if the tomatoes begin to dry out.
11. Whisk together the tahini, lemon juice, maple syrup, garlic, cumin, salt, and pepper. Whisk in the ice water 1 tbsp at a time until the sauce is creamy and pourable.
12. Top the cabbage with the herbs and a squeeze of lemon. Spoon tahini sauce over the cabbage and serve the remaining sauce alongside.

## Notes

* Add a drained can of chickpeas with the tomatoes to make the dish more substantial.
* Serve with cooked grains or thick slices of bread.

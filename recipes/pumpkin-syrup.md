# Pumpkin syrup

Source: [kale — pumpkin syrup](https://www.tiktok.com/@supimkale/video/7663591157808516366), shared by Jill.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup maple syrup (or another 1/2 cup brown sugar)
- 1 cup plain pumpkin purée, not pumpkin pie filling
- 2 cups water for thinner syrup; use 1 cup for a thicker sauce
- 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tsp vanilla bean paste

## Instructions
1. Combine everything EXCEPT the vanilla bean paste in a saucepan.
2. Simmer over low heat for 10 minutes.
3. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla bean paste.
4. Transfer to jars/containers and refrigerate once cooled.
5. Shake or stir before using in drinks.

Creator’s storage guidance: up to 2 weeks refrigerated.
The listed ingredients are plant-based; choose vegan-labeled brown sugar if desired.

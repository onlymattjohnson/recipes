# Miso Garlic Melting Cabbage

Deeply seared cabbage wedges baked until tender in a creamy miso, garlic, and chili sauce.

Serves 4.

Source: [Andrew Bernard's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w0lP6rWSHfA) and [Make It Dairy Free recipe](https://makeitdairyfree.com/miso-garlic-melting-cabbage/)

## Ingredients

### Cashew Cream

* 1/2 cup raw cashews
* 1/2 cup water

### Cabbage

* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 medium green cabbage (2 to 2 1/2 lb / no more than 1,200g), core intact and cut into 4 wedges
* 3 tbsp vegan butter
* 1 shallot, finely minced
* 8 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* 1/2 tsp dried thyme
* 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
* 1/4 tsp salt, plus more to taste
* 1/4 tsp ground black pepper, plus more to taste
* 1 1/2 cups low- or no-sodium vegetable broth, divided
* 1 1/2 tbsp white miso paste
* 1/2 cup freshly grated vegan Parmesan-style cheese (40g)
* 2 tbsp chili oil, or to taste
* 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Blend the cashews and water in a high-powered blender until smooth and creamy. Set aside.
3. Heat the olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Add the cabbage wedges cut-side down and sear for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until deeply golden. Transfer to a plate.
4. Reduce the heat to medium-low. Melt the vegan butter in the same skillet, then add the shallot and garlic. Cook for about 2 minutes, until softened and fragrant.
5. Add the garlic powder, thyme, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Stir for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.
6. Pour in 1 1/4 cups vegetable broth and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the skillet. Simmer for 1 to 2 minutes.
7. Whisk the miso into the remaining 1/4 cup broth. Add it to the skillet with the cashew cream, vegan Parmesan, and chili oil.
8. Simmer the sauce for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring, until smooth and lightly thickened.
9. Return the cabbage wedges to the skillet and spoon sauce over them.
10. Bake uncovered for 45 to 55 minutes, until the cabbage is very tender and the sauce is bubbling and lightly golden.
11. Spoon more sauce over the cabbage, garnish with parsley, and adjust the seasoning before serving.

## Notes

* If the cabbage weighs more than 2 1/2 lb (1,200g), cut it into 8 wedges or parboil or steam it before searing so it cooks through.
* If the skillet is not oven-safe, transfer the sauce and cabbage to a 9-by-13-inch baking dish before baking.
* For a nut-free sauce, replace the cashew cream with blended pepitas, coconut cream, or extra-creamy oat milk.
* Replace white miso with 1 tbsp red miso and omit the added salt until tasting, or use 1 1/2 tbsp chickpea miso.
* Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for 4 to 5 days.

# Apple crisp syrup

Source: [Lacey — apple crisp syrup](https://www.tiktok.com/@laceyglasley/video/7675707496777911566), shared by Jill.

## Ingredients
- 3 medium apples or 2 large apples
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon (creator also offers cinnamon baking syrup)
- 1/2 cup apple cider or apple juice
- 1/2 cup water
- Optional extra cinnamon to taste

## Instructions
1. Add apples, brown sugar and cinnamon to a pot over medium heat; let the sugar begin to melt.
2. Add water and cider/juice. Bring to a simmer and cook 15–20 minutes, until the apples are very soft. The creator emphasizes allowing enough time to extract the apple flavor.
3. Let cool and add more cinnamon if desired.
4. Transfer to a container and refrigerate.

Caption does not specify apple variety, peeling, chopping or straining. Practical preparation suggestion: core and chop the apples; optionally strain for a smooth drink syrup. These are additions, not instructions stated in the caption.
Creator’s storage guidance: 2–3 weeks refrigerated.
The listed ingredients are plant-based; choose vegan-labeled brown sugar if desired.

# Rice Krispy treats

Classic rice crispy treats — vegan fall version
Adapted from the [original Rice Krispies stovetop recipe](https://www.ricekrispies.us/original-treats-saucepan), using vegan ingredients and Trader Joe’s fall-flavored marshmallows.

## Ingredients — one 9 × 13-inch pan
- 3 tablespoons (about 42 g) vegan butter sticks, plus a little for greasing.
- 10 oz (283 g) Trader Joe’s fall-flavored vegan marshmallows — maple or pumpkin, whichever you pick up. Weigh them rather than counting pieces; buy enough bags to total at least 10 oz. This is the same marshmallow purchase as the separate Trader Joe’s marshmallows task.
- 6 cups vegan crispy rice cereal, such as [Nature’s Path Organic Crispy Rice](https://naturespath.com/collections/natures-path/products/crispy-rice-cold-cereal-np-us), which the manufacturer labels vegan.

## Instructions
1. Line a 9 × 13-inch pan with parchment and lightly grease it with vegan butter.
2. Melt the 3 tablespoons vegan butter in a large pot over low heat. Add the marshmallows and stir patiently until melted together. Cut large marshmallows into smaller pieces first if helpful; keep the heat low.
3. Remove from heat. Fold in the cereal until evenly coated.
4. Transfer promptly to the pan. Use a greased spatula or parchment to press gently into an even layer; avoid packing tightly.
5. Let cool completely, then cut into squares. Best enjoyed the day they’re made; keep leftovers airtight.

The fall flavor comes from the marshmallows, so no extra syrup or spices are needed. These substitutions adapt the classic recipe; the exact Trader Joe’s seasonal-marshmallow version has not been tested here.

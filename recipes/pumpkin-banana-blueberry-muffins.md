# Moist Pumpkin–Banana Blueberry Muffins

Vegan muffins with a moist crumb. Makes about 12.

Source: ChatGPT recipe from September 20, 2026.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup whole-wheat flour
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup oat flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger (optional)
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg (optional)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup mashed ripe banana
- 3/4 cup pumpkin purée
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1/3 cup neutral oil
- 1/4 cup unsweetened plant milk, plus 1–2 tablespoons only if needed
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries

### Optional topping

- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Grease or line a 12-cup muffin pan.
2. In a bowl, whisk together the flours, baking powder, cinnamon, optional ginger and nutmeg, and salt.
3. In another bowl, whisk together the banana, pumpkin, brown sugar, oil, plant milk, and vanilla.
4. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients just until combined, then fold in the blueberries. The batter should be thick and scoopable. If it is too stiff, add only 1–2 tablespoons more plant milk. Use frozen blueberries straight from the freezer.
5. Divide the batter among the muffin cups, filling each about three-quarters full to nearly full. If using the topping, mix the sugar and cinnamon and sprinkle over the muffins.
6. Bake for 23–28 minutes, until the tops are set and a tester inserted away from blueberry pockets comes out without wet batter (moist crumbs are fine).
7. Cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack for another 15–20 minutes.

## Notes

- Avoid adding extra pumpkin purée; it can make the muffins heavy.
